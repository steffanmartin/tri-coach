## The limiter

Working from 270 W FTP, 3:25/km threshold, 1:40/100 m CSS, a ~sub-10 target
implies roughly:

| Leg | Target | What it demands |
|---|---|---|
| Swim 3.8 km | ~1:10 | ~1:50/100 m in a wetsuit — comfortably inside current CSS |
| Bike 180 km | ~5:20 | ~185 W NP = **0.68 × FTP held for five and a half hours** |
| Run 42.2 km | ~3:10 | ~20% slower than a standalone marathon you have already run |

The run is **not** the limiter. The swim is **not** the limiter. The limiter is
holding ~0.68 IF for 5+ hours and still being able to run off it — durability,
position/aerodynamics, and gut tolerance. Twelve months of training should be
weighted accordingly: bike volume up, run volume held roughly where it is and
made more durable, swim on technique plus enough continuous volume that 3.8 km
is unremarkable.

Raising FTP helps less than raising the fraction of FTP you can hold while
absorbing 100 g of carbohydrate an hour.

## Block structure (~50 weeks)

| Block | Weeks | h/wk | TSS/wk | CTL at exit | Defining sessions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prep | 1–4 | 8–10 | 400–500 | ~60 | Swim technique reset, strength foundation, easy volume |
| Base 1 | 5–12 | 10–12 | 550–650 | ~72 | 4 rides/wk, long ride to 3 h, 3 swims, 2× heavy strength |
| Base 2 | 13–20 | 11–13 | 600–700 | ~82 | Sweet-spot intro, long run to 1:45, CSS swim sets |
| Build 1 | 21–30 | 12–15 | 700–820 | ~95 | SST blocks, weekly brick, long ride to 4 h, fuel to 75 g/h |
| Build 2 | 31–40 | 14–17 | 800–950 | ~108 | IM-watts rides 4–5 h, long run 2:15–2:30, open water, TT position, fuel to 90 g/h |
| Peak | 41–47 | 16–19 | 900–1050 | ~118 | 5–6 h rides, **race-sim day**, fuel 100–110 g/h |
| Taper | 48–50 | 60/45/30% of peak | — | ~110 | Volume down, intensity frequency held |

Recovery week every 4th week in base, every 3rd in build and peak: 55–65% volume,
same number of quality sessions, shorter.

## Anchor events
- **Half-distance tune-up, 8–12 weeks out.** Full fuelling and pacing rehearsal in
  race kit. Not an A race — a dress rehearsal you happen to race.
- **Race-simulation day, 5–7 weeks out.** ~4.5 h at IM watts, straight into 60–75
  min at target run pace, exact race-day nutrition and kit.

## Fuelling progression
| Block | g CHO/hr on long rides |
|---|---|
| Base | 60 |
| Build 1 | 75 |
| Build 2 | 90 |
| Peak | 100–110 |

Practise on every ride over 2 h. Race-day intake is never higher than what has
been rehearsed.
